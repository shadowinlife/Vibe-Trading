## 7. Limitations

This is an 11-item bounded probe, not the 40-item gate: the paired table uses the frozen ten that also anchor the baseline, and `c27` is added only for the closing and timeout-recovery evidence. McNemar p-values at n=10 have low power and are reported for audit, not as significance claims. Route defects visible in this probe (c06 executed instead of clarifying; c09 executed a prediction instead of answering; c10/c27 misrouted by the refusal-first classifier) are recorded, not repaired here.
